     What to do
     ──────────

     Bind the same names in every alternative, or split this into separate arms.

     Why
     ───

     One arm has one body, and it can name only what every alternative supplies,
     at the same type. An alternative that binds y where another binds x leaves
     the body with a name that is sometimes missing.
