  = a spread's binding is replaced by an explicit one, but two explicit bindings of one effect are a mistake

    What to do
    ──────────

    Delete one of the two bindings.

    Why
    ───

    An explicit binding replaces a spread's binding: context { ..Fixture(),
    FsRead: fs().files([]) } is how a test overrides a default. Two explicit
    bindings of one effect have no such reading, so the later one does not
    silently win.
