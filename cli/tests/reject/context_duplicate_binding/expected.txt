    What to do
    ──────────

    Delete one of the two bindings.

    Why
    ───

    An explicit binding replaces a spread's binding: context { ..Fixture(),
    FileSystemRead: fs().files([]) } is how a test overrides a default. Two
    explicit bindings have no such reading, so the later one does not silently
    win.
