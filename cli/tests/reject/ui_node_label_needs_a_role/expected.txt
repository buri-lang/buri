   = fix: give the stack a `role`, or drop the `label`

     A label is the name a reader hears for what a stack is. A stack with no
     role is a plain box a reader isn't told about, so there's nothing to name.
     Give it the role it plays:

       fn chart<C>(bars: [Node<C>]): Node<C> {
           ui.stack({
               styles: [.Layout(.Layers)],
               children: bars,
               role: .Some(.Group),
               label: .Some(.Const("Value over the past year, from 12 to 40")),
           })
       }

     A decorative stack can't take a label either: it's hidden from a reader,
     and it takes no role (`decorative-with-role` (decorative-with-role.md)).

     The compiler reads both fields where the stack is written, in the call or
     not. A pair it can't read is refused too, because it can't tell that the
     stack has a role.
