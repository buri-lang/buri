   = fix: move the `load` onto the press or the route that reaches this subtree, and hand the builder what it loaded

     ui.computed, ui.each and ui.rebuild call their closure to build a subtree.
     load waits for a chunk, which makes the closure async: it returns a
     promise, not a node. The page comes up blank, and the first failed render
     leaves the rest of it inert.

     A handler, fn(C, Event) => (), may wait: it runs to completion and writes
     signals when the wait is over. So load goes on the press or route that
     decides the subtree is wanted, and the builder reads what it loaded from a
     signal. On a website, that's the route match.
