     What to do
     ──────────

     Make Inner satisfy Equal first — derive Equal for Inner; in its own module,
     or an impl — or drop Equal from this derive.

     Why
     ───

     A derive is only ever as available as the components it is built from,
     which is why the diagnostic names the component rather than the type you
     wrote it on.
