     What to do
     ──────────

     Make Inner satisfy Eq first — derive Eq for Inner; in its own module, or an
     impl — or drop Eq from this derive.

     Why
     ───

     A derived implementation is exactly the fold: Outer's eq is its fields' eq.
     So a derive is only ever as available as the components it is built from,
     and the diagnostic names the component rather than the type you wrote it
     on.
