  = fix: delete it, or move it above the arm that subsumes it

    An arm is unreachable when the arms before it cover it, or when no value
    fits it at all, like .Some(_) on an Option<Never>. A guarded arm covers
    nothing, because its guard may fail. The usual cause is an arm in the wrong
    place, and a dead arm reads as handled.
