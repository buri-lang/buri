     What to do
     ──────────

     Correct the spelling, or name a field the type declares. Where there is a
     near miss the fix names it: "if you meant f0, use that; if not, Rec's
     declaration lists its fields".

     Why
     ───

     There is no structural typing and no inheritance. A value's fields are
     exactly the ones its declaration lists, which is how the diagnostic can
     offer the nearest name the type does have.
