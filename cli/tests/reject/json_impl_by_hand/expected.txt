     What to do
     ──────────

     Write derive ToJson for Point; instead.

     Why
     ───

     A derived encoder stands for the type's *shape*, and one walker in the
     runtime reads it. So json.encode(ctx, date) would call a hand-written impl
     ToJson for Date, while json.encode(ctx, appointment) would walk straight
     past it, because Appointment holds a Date and derives its own. One value,
     two encodings.

     A type that needs a different document is a type you convert to first,
     through a function you can see at the call site. core/json's ToJson and
     FromJson are the only two traits this applies to.
