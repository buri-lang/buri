     The usual cause is a pattern in the wrong place, and dead text in an arm
     reads as handled. An arm with no live alternative is an unreachable-arm
     instead.

     Only the arm's top-level | alternatives are reported. An alternation nested
     in a constructor, as in .Some(true | false), counts toward coverage but
     isn't reported branch by branch.
