     What to do
     ──────────

     Delete the alternative. The arm keeps the ones that still do something, so
     nothing else about it changes. The usual cause is a pattern in the wrong
     place, and dead text in an arm reads as handled.

     An arm with no live alternative at all is one unreachable-arm instead. The
     alternatives in question are the arm's own, separated by | at the top of
     its pattern; an alternation nested inside a constructor, as in .Some(true |
     false), counts toward coverage but is not reported branch by branch.
