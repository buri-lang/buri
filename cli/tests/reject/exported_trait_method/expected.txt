   = fix: drop the `export`

     What to do
     ──────────

     Drop the export.

     Why
     ───

     Conformance belongs to the type. Once Version is visible, everything impl
     Eq for Version supplies is visible with it, so export has nothing left to
     decide. Withholding a method the trait requires would be a conformance that
     does not hold.

     A program that provokes it
     ──────────────────────────

     An impl block for the type's own methods is the other case, and export
     means something there.
