     What to do
     ──────────

     Write the qualified form — Option.Some(...) — or annotate what this value
     is being used as.

     Why
     ───

     .Some is shorthand for "the Some of whatever type is expected here", and a
     let with no annotation expects nothing. Inference flows into the shorthand,
     never out of it, which is what leaves two enums free to share a variant
     name.
