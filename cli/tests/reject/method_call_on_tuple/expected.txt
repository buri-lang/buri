    What to do
    ──────────

    Check the spelling, or declare it in an impl block in that type's own
    module.

    Why
    ───

    A method is looked up in exactly one place: the module that declares the
    receiver's type. There is no extension mechanism, so a method cannot be
    added to a type from outside — which is also what makes resolution a single
    lookup rather than a search.
