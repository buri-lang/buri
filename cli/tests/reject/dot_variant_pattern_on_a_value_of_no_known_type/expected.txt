    .Variant means "that variant of whatever type is expected here", and a let
    with no annotation expects nothing. Inference flows into the shorthand,
    never out of it, so two enums can share a variant name.

    A closure's answer expects nothing either, unless something gives the
    closure a function type, such as an annotated let or the parameter it's
    passed to.
