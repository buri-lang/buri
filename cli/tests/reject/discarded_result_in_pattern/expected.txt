     A Result can be thrown away in only two places: bound to _ in a let, or
     left as an expression statement. Both are this error, so .ignore() is the
     one spelling of a deliberate drop, and buri lint reports it as
     discarded-result.

     A _ anywhere in the pattern counts, so let (count, _) = (1, mayFail()); is
     this error too.
