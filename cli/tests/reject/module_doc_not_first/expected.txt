  = fix: move it above the first declaration, or write `///` to document the declaration below it

    What to do
    ──────────

    Move it above the first declaration, or write /// to document the
    declaration below it.

    Why
    ───

    /// attaches downward, to the declaration beneath it. //! attaches upward,
    to whatever contains it — at the top of a file, the module. Lower down it
    has nothing to attach to, so it is almost always a /// typo.
