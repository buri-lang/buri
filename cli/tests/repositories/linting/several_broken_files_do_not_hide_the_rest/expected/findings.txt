   = a binding nothing reads is either a computation whose result is dead or a value that was meant to be wired in and was not

     An unused local is usually one of three things:

     - A result nobody consumes. Delete the computation, not just the binding.
     - A leftover from a refactor.
     - A value you meant to use and forgot to wire in. That's the real bug.

     Decide which before deleting. If the right-hand side has side effects you
     need, keep the call and drop the binding. If the value was meant to be
     returned or passed on, finish that instead of silencing the warning.
